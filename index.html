Privacy Policy for Personal Notes

App name: Personal Notes

Developer: Abhay Bhagat

Contact email: abhaybhagat170@gmail.com

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Personal Notes (the "App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App has two parts: a notes part and an attendance part (attendance, reports, people and payroll for a factory, site, class or team). This policy covers both and is written to reflect what the App actually does.

Summary

  • Your notes, attachments, and app-lock password are stored only on your device. Note titles and note text are never uploaded to us. There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Attendance data is stored on your device. If you keep cloud sync turned on for an attendance workspace (it is on by default when you create one), that workspace's members, attendance records, shifts, payroll adjustments and edit history are also stored in Google Firebase so that other people who have your share code can see and edit them. You can turn cloud sync off or delete the workspace from the cloud at any time.
  • The App sends usage analytics (a random device ID, the name you entered in the App, app version, session time, and counts such as how many notes you have) to our backend, Google Firebase.
  • Our backend can also send the App update rules, maintenance notices, and announcements, and can pause the App for a specific install.
  • If you use Send feedback, your message (and the optional email or phone you type) is sent to us.
  • If you use Translate on selected text, that text is sent to Google Translate. If you use voice dictation, your device's speech recognizer processes your speech, which may be done by Google's servers.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising identifiers (see Section 7).
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Notes information stored only on your device (never sent to us)

  • Notes content — titles, text, checklists, tables, sketches, colors/backgrounds, fonts, and formatting, stored locally using an on-device database (Hive).
  • Attachments — photos, videos, voice memos, and links you add to a note, saved to the App's local storage on your device.
  • App-lock password — if you set a password to open the App or lock a note, only a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of it is stored on your device. We never see or store your actual password, and it is not sent anywhere.
  • Device screen-lock check — if you forget your app password, the App can ask your phone to confirm it is you (screen PIN, pattern, password, or fingerprint) before letting you reset it. This check is performed by your phone's operating system. The App does not read or store your PIN, pattern, or biometric data.
  • Reminders — dates/times you set for note reminders are stored locally and used only to schedule notifications on your device. A reminder notification shows the note's title and the first part of its text on your own device only.
  • App settings and preferences (theme, sort order, unlocked fonts, read announcements, onboarding status, etc.).

The App has no accounts and no sign-in. It never uploads your notes or attachments. Files leave your device only when you choose to export, share, print, or back them up (see Section 1.7).

1.2 Attendance information

The attendance part of the App lets you create a workspace (for example a factory, site, school class or team), add the people you track, and record their attendance. Depending on what you choose to enter, this can include:

  • People: name, ID / roll number, group or class, phone number, role or designation, pay type and pay rate, active status, and shift assignment.
  • Attendance records: for each person and day — status (present, late, half day, leave, absent, holiday), check-in and check-out times, notes, and the shift used.
  • Workspace settings: workspace name and type, working days, shift times, holidays, late and overtime rules, currency, and an optional PIN used to lock past days (only a hash of the PIN is stored).
  • Payroll data: pay rates, bonuses, deductions or advances you add, and calculated pay for a month.
  • Edit history: a log of changes (what changed, when, and by whom) so edits to locked days are recorded.
  • QR codes: each person can have a QR ID card generated from their ID code. The App's camera scanner reads these codes to check people in.

This information is about the people you track. You are responsible for having a lawful basis and any required permission (for example from your employees, or from parents or guardians if you track students or minors) before entering their information. We do not collect biometric data, photos of people, or location for attendance.

1.3 Attendance cloud sync (sent to Firebase)

When you create or join a workspace with cloud sync on, the attendance information in Section 1.2 (people, groups, shifts, attendance records, payroll adjustments, workspace settings including the PIN hash, and edit history) is copied to Google Firebase Realtime Database under a long, random share code. Please understand the following:

  • The share code is the access key. Anyone who has the code can join the workspace, read its data, and make changes. Share it only with people you trust, and treat it like a password.
  • There are no individual user accounts or per-person permissions inside a workspace, so everyone with the code has the same access.
  • Data is sent over an encrypted connection. We do not separately encrypt the contents of a workspace on top of that.
  • You can turn cloud sync off in the workspace settings; the cloud copy is then kept (so others can still use it) until you remove it. You can also choose to delete the workspace from the cloud, which erases all of its members and attendance records from Firebase for everyone.
  • If you turn cloud sync off when you create a workspace, its data stays on your device only.

1.4 Usage analytics (sent to Firebase)

When you use the App, it connects to our backend, Google Firebase Realtime Database, and sends usage data. We use this to understand how many people use the App, how often, and on which versions. Data is tied to a randomly generated device ID created on your device the first time you open the App. It is not your advertising ID or any hardware identifier, and it resets if you reinstall the App. The App also sends the name you enter in the App (for example when you first set it up), so we can tell users apart.

The data sent includes:

  • Device and app details — random device ID, the name you entered, app version and build number, operating system (e.g. Android), your device's language setting, and whether you use the light or dark theme.
  • Session data — when you open the App, the time of your last use, the number of sessions, and total and daily time spent in the App (recorded roughly every minute while the App is open).
  • Install information — when the App was first installed. If you updated from an older version that already had notes, we use the creation date of your oldest note as the approximate install date, and we record that you were an existing user.
  • Note activity events — when a note is created, edited, or deleted, we log the event type, whether it was a note or a checklist, the word count, and a timestamp. Edits are logged at most about once a minute per note.
  • Aggregate counts — your total notes, notes in the trash, archived, pinned, locked, checklist, tables, markdown, linked notes, notes with attachments, notes with reminders, and your total word count; the time of your most recent note change; and the number of times you have exported or imported backups.
  • A yes/no flag for whether you have set an app password.
  • Attendance workspace summary — for each attendance workspace you use, we receive only summary information: the workspace name and type, your name as owner, the number of members and groups, how many attendance entries were marked today and in the last 7 days, the total number of records, whether cloud sync is on, the date it was created, the time it was last active, your device ID, and the app version. This summary does not include member names or attendance details (those are only in the workspace data described in Section 1.3, and only if cloud sync is on).

We do not use analytics to read note titles, note text, attachments, or your password. Analytics data is not used for advertising or profiling. If the App is offline, this data is simply skipped or delayed.

1.5 Remote controls and announcements

While the App is open, it checks our Firebase backend about once a minute for:

  • App-wide settings such as the minimum or latest version, maintenance mode, and update links, which may require you to update the App before continuing;
  • A setting that can pause the App for a specific install (identified by its random device ID), with an optional message;
  • Announcements and notices, either sent to all users (optionally filtered by platform or app version) or to one specific install.

When an announcement is delivered to or opened on your device, the App records a delivered or read timestamp together with your random device ID so we can tell whether it reached you. Announcements may include a link, which opens in your browser or another app only when you tap it.

1.6 Feedback you send

If you use Settings → Send feedback, the App sends us: the feedback type you pick, your star rating, your message, the optional email or phone number you choose to type, your random device ID, app version, platform, and a timestamp. Please do not include sensitive information in your message. We use feedback only to improve the App and, if you gave contact details, to reply to you.

1.7 Exports, backups, reports, and sharing (you control these)

  • Export / backup / PDF — you can export notes as backup files or PDFs, which are saved to your device's Downloads folder (in a "SmartNotes" subfolder) or opened in your device's share/print screen.
  • Attendance reports — you can create attendance reports, yearly summaries, payroll sheets (CSV), payslips, report cards and QR ID cards as PDF or CSV files and share or print them.
  • Import — you can import a backup file you pick using your device's file picker.
  • Share — when you tap Share on a note, report, or file, your device's share sheet opens and the content goes to the app you choose. Once shared, it is governed by that app's privacy policy, not ours. Reports and payslips contain personal and pay information, so share them carefully.

2. Translate and Voice Dictation

  • Translate — when you select text in a note and tap Translate, only the selected text is sent over the internet to Google's translation service to produce the translation. This happens only when you use the feature, and requires an internet connection. Google handles that text under its own privacy policy.
  • Voice dictation — when you tap the dictation button, the App uses your device's built-in speech recognizer. Depending on your device and settings, your speech may be processed on-device or sent to the speech service provided by your device maker or Google. The App itself does not keep or upload your audio and only receives the recognized text. (This is different from voice memos, which are recorded audio files saved on your device.)

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Provide core app functionality (storing and organizing your notes and attendance data, and syncing attendance workspaces you choose to sync);
  • Understand how the App is used — number of users, how often and how long it is used, versions in use, and which features are used (Section 1.4);
  • Manage the App — require updates, announce maintenance or new features, deliver notices, and pause the App for an install if needed (Section 1.5);
  • Read and respond to feedback you send (Section 1.6);
  • Serve and measure advertising, as described in Section 7.

We do not use your information for our own advertising or profiling, and we do not operate any crash-reporting SDKs in the App.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Realtime Database) — our backend for usage analytics, attendance cloud sync, remote controls and announcements, and feedback (Sections 1.3–1.6). Firebase stores this data on our behalf and processes it according to Google's policies: firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Data may be stored and processed in the United States or other countries where Google operates.
  • Google Translate — used only when you choose to translate selected text (Section 2).
  • Google AdMob — used to display in-app advertising (Section 7).
  • Your device's speech recognition service — used only when you choose voice dictation (Section 2).

We do not share your information with data brokers, and we do not sell your personal information.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Security

  • Local data (notes, attachments, attendance data, password and PIN hashes, settings) is stored in the App's private storage area on your device and is not accessible to other apps. Uninstalling the App deletes it, unless you exported files to your Downloads folder or the attendance workspace is also stored in the cloud.
  • Analytics data, attendance workspaces that use cloud sync, remote-control records, and feedback are stored on Firebase's infrastructure. We keep this data for as long as it is useful for running and improving the App, or until you delete it or ask us to delete it (Section 8).
  • App-lock passwords and attendance PINs are never stored or transmitted in plain text.
  • Data sent to Firebase is transmitted over an encrypted connection. Access to a synced attendance workspace depends on knowing its share code (Section 1.3).

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Employer / Organizer Responsibility

If you use the attendance part of the App to record other people (employees, workers, students, or members), you decide what information is entered and who gets the share code. You are responsible for informing those people, obtaining any consent required by the laws that apply to you, keeping their information accurate, and deleting it when it is no longer needed. People whose attendance is recorded should contact the person or organization that runs their workspace about their data.

7. Advertising

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob, including banner ads in the note editor, full-screen (interstitial) ads, app-open ads when you return to the App, and rewarded ads (for example when unlocking a premium font or locking a note). To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your advertising identifier (e.g. Android Advertising ID) and general device information (device model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and engagement with ads shown in the App).

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, according to Google's own privacy and advertising policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from partner sites/apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can:

  • Opt out of personalized ads and reset your advertising identifier in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (Android) or equivalent;
  • Manage your Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Notes content, attachments, attendance data, and the usage-analytics data described in Section 1.4 are not shared with AdMob or any advertiser. Only the device and advertising data described above, collected directly by the AdMob SDK, is involved.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Core note-taking features and on-device attendance work fully offline. Without a connection, analytics, announcements, attendance cloud sync, feedback, and translation simply won't work until you reconnect.
  • You can deny any individual device permission (camera, microphone, photos, notifications); the related feature simply won't be available.
  • You can export or delete your notes locally at any time.
  • You can turn off cloud sync for an attendance workspace, leave a workspace, or delete the workspace from the cloud in the attendance settings. Deleting it from the cloud erases its members and attendance records for everyone who uses it.
  • You can reset your analytics identity by uninstalling and reinstalling the App, which creates a new random device ID.
  • You can ask us to delete the data associated with you — usage analytics linked to your device ID, attendance workspace summaries, and any feedback you sent — by emailing abhaybhagat170@gmail.com. Because analytics are tied to a random ID and not to your identity, we may need you to include your device ID, workspace share code or name, the contact details you used in feedback, or other details to help us find your records.
  • If you are a person whose attendance is recorded in someone else's workspace, please contact the owner of that workspace first; you may also write to us at the email above and we will help where we can.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising as described in Section 7.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own purposes. The App's ads are not configured to be child-directed, and we do not knowingly serve personalized ads to children. A school, teacher or other organizer may use the attendance part to record students; in that case the organizer is responsible for obtaining any required parent or guardian permission (see Section 6). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information (for example, through the feedback form), contact us at abhaybhagat170@gmail.com and we will delete it.
