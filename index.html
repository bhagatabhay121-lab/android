Privacy Policy for Notes

App name: Smart Notes - Tasks & Voice

Developer: Abhay Bhagat

Contact email: abhaybhagat170@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information Notes collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It is written to reflect exactly what the app does — nothing more, nothing less.

Summary

  • Your notes, attachments, and app-lock password are stored only on your device unless you choose to publish a note.
  • The optional AI Assistant runs entirely on your device — your notes are never sent to us or to any AI company to generate a response.
  • If you create an account or publish a note, that specific content is sent to our backend provider, Supabase, to make sharing possible.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects device/advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads (see Section 6).
  • We do not use any analytics or tracking SDKs beyond the ad SDK described below, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information stored only on your device (never sent to us)

  • Notes content — titles, text, checklists, tables, colors/backgrounds, and formatting, stored locally using an on-device database (Hive).
  • Attachments — photos, videos, and voice memos you add to a note, saved to the app's local storage folder on your device.
  • App-lock password — if you set a password to open the app, only a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of it is stored on your device. We never see or store your actual password, and it is not sent anywhere.
  • Reminders — dates/times you set for note reminders are stored locally and used only to schedule notifications on your device.
  • App settings and preferences (theme, sort order, onboarding status, etc.).

1.2 Device permissions

The App requests the following permissions, only for the features you actively use:

PermissionWhy it's requested
MicrophoneTo record voice memo attachments you choose to add to a note
CameraTo let you take a photo directly from within the App to attach to a note
Photos / Videos / StorageTo let you pick existing images or videos to attach to a note, and to save/export/open files (e.g. PDF export)
NotificationsTo show local reminder notifications for notes you've scheduled, and for the optional "Quick Assistant" notification shortcut

Media and audio you capture stay on your device unless it is part of a note you explicitly publish (attachments themselves are not uploaded when you publish — only the note's text content is).

2. The On-Device AI Assistant

The App includes an optional AI Assistant that helps you create, edit, find, and organize notes using natural language.

  • The assistant runs a language model entirely on your device (downloaded once from Hugging Face, a public model-hosting service, the first time you enable the feature).
  • Your notes and prompts to the assistant are processed locally and are not transmitted to us or to any AI company.
  • The only network activity related to this feature is the one-time download of the model file itself, which does not include any of your personal data.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Provide core app functionality (storing and organizing your notes locally);
  • Operate optional features you choose to use (accounts, publishing/sharing notes, reminders, attachments);
  • Authenticate you and secure your account, if you create one;
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the publishing/sharing service;
  • Serve and measure advertising, as described in Section 6.

We do not use your information for our own advertising or profiling, and we do not operate any analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the App.

4. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party service providers:

  • Hugging Face (used only to download the AI model file; no personal or note data is sent).
  • Google AdMob (used to display in-app advertising; see Section 6).

We do not share your information with data brokers, and we do not sell your personal information.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Local data (notes, attachments, password hash, settings) is stored in the App's private storage area on your device and is not accessible to other apps.
  • App-lock passwords and account passwords are never stored or transmitted in plain text.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Advertising

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your advertising identifier (e.g. Android Advertising ID) and general device information (device model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and engagement with ads shown in the App).

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, according to Google's own privacy and advertising policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from partner sites/apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Depending on your region (e.g. the EEA, UK, and other jurisdictions with similar requirements), you may be shown a consent prompt before ads are personalized, and you can choose whether to allow personalized advertising. You can also:

  • Opt out of personalized ads and reset your advertising identifier in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (Android) or equivalent;
  • Manage your Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Notes content, attachments, and account information are not shared with AdMob or any advertiser — only the device/advertising data described above, collected directly by the AdMob SDK, is involved.

7. Your Choices

  • You can use the App entirely offline.
  • You can deny any individual device permission (camera, microphone, photos, notifications); the related feature simply won't be available.
  • You can export or delete your notes locally at any time.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising as described in Section 6.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App's ads are not configured to be child-directed, and we do not knowingly serve personalized ads to children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at abhaybhagat170@gmail.com and we will delete it.
